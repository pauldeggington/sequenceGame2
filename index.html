Strategy & Tactics

The Corner Strategy

The four corners (★) are the most valuable real estate on the board because they are permanent free spaces that cannot be removed.

  • Multi-Directional: A corner can anchor sequences horizontally, vertically, AND diagonally at the same time.
  • Shorter Sequences: Since the corner counts as your color, you only need 4 more chips to complete a 5-chip sequence.
  • Claim Early: Prioritize playing cards that land near corners or using Two-Eyed Jacks to secure a corner spot before your opponent can block the surrounding paths.

The Wipe: Tactical Destruction

Properly timing The Wipe can turn a guaranteed loss into a winning opening.

  • Break the Lock: Use it to clear a row or column where an opponent is one chip away from a sequence.
  • Clear Deadlocks: If a section of the board is filled with mixed chips that serve no one, wipe it to reset the field.
  • The Ultimate Sacrifice: Giving up two wild Jacks is a high cost. Only trigger it when the destruction of an opponent's progress outweighs the value of two free placements.
